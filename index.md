# Privacy Policy for BGI

**Effective Date:** 04 August 2026

**App Name:** BGI
**Organization:** Buddha Group of Institutions
**App Purpose:** School/Institution Management System

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to **BGI**, the official school and institution management application of **Buddha Group of Institutions** (“BGI”, “we”, “us”, or “our”).

BGI is designed to provide students, parents/guardians, teachers, staff, and authorized administrators with access to institution-related services and information.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, protect, and disclose information when you use the BGI mobile application.

By using the BGI application, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

## 2. Information We Collect

Depending on your role and the features you use, BGI may collect and process the following information.

### 2.1 Student Information

The application may contain or process:

* Student name
* Admission/registration number
* Date of birth
* Gender
* Student photograph
* Father's name
* Mother's name
* Guardian information
* Mobile number
* Email address
* Residential address
* Class and section
* Academic information
* Attendance records
* Examination and assessment records
* Fee and payment records
* School/institution information
* Event and activity information

### 2.2 Parent/Guardian Information

For parents or guardians, the application may process:

* Name
* Mobile number
* Email address
* Relationship with student
* Login information
* Student-related information
* Communication and notification information

### 2.3 Teacher and Staff Information

For authorized teachers and staff, BGI may process:

* Name
* Employee/staff ID
* Mobile number
* Email address
* Designation
* Department
* Attendance information
* Login information
* Role and permissions
* Institution-related information

### 2.4 Account Information

To provide secure access to the application, we may process:

* Mobile number, username, or email address
* Password/authentication information
* User role
* Account status
* Login/session information

## 3. How We Use Information

Information collected through BGI may be used for legitimate educational and administrative purposes, including:

* Managing student records
* Managing parent/guardian accounts
* Managing teacher and staff accounts
* Recording attendance
* Managing academic information
* Managing examinations and results
* Managing school/institution fees
* Sending fee reminders
* Sending attendance notifications
* Sending examination and academic notifications
* Sending announcements and notices
* Managing school events
* Providing educational information
* Generating administrative reports
* Providing customer and technical support
* Maintaining application security
* Preventing unauthorized access
* Improving application performance and functionality

We do not sell student or parent personal information.

## 4. Notifications

BGI may send notifications related to the school or institution, including:

* Attendance notifications
* Fee reminders
* Examination notifications
* Academic updates
* School announcements
* Events and activities
* Important notices
* Emergency communications
* Account-related notifications

Notifications may be delivered through push notifications, SMS, WhatsApp, email, or other communication services configured by Buddha Group of Institutions.

## 5. Attendance Information

BGI may provide attendance management features.

Attendance information may include:

* Student/staff identification
* Attendance date
* Attendance time
* Attendance status
* Attendance type
* Device or technical information associated with attendance

Attendance information is used solely for authorized educational and administrative purposes.

## 6. Camera and Photo Access

The application may request camera or photo-library access for features such as:

* Student photographs
* Staff photographs
* Profile photographs
* Document uploads
* QR code scanning
* Attendance-related functionality

Camera or photo access will only be requested when required by the relevant feature.

## 7. QR Code Scanning

If QR-code functionality is enabled, BGI may use the device camera to scan QR codes for authorized purposes, such as attendance or identification.

QR-code information may be processed together with the relevant student or staff account and attendance information.

## 8. Location Information

If a location-based feature is enabled in BGI, the application may request access to device location information.

Location information may be used for authorized institution-related features such as attendance verification or location-based services.

If location access is not required for a particular feature, the application will not request it for that purpose.

## 9. Payment Information

BGI may provide online fee-payment functionality through third-party payment service providers.

When a payment is made, we may receive information such as:

* Transaction ID
* Payment reference number
* Amount
* Payment status
* Payment date and time

Payment service providers may process payment information according to their own privacy policies and security practices.

BGI does not intend to directly store sensitive payment credentials such as:

* Credit/debit card CVV
* UPI PIN
* Internet banking passwords

Such information is handled by the applicable payment provider.

## 10. Push Notifications

BGI may use third-party push notification services, including **Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM)**, to send notifications to registered devices.

These services may process device-related identifiers and technical information necessary to deliver notifications.

## 11. SMS, WhatsApp and Email

BGI may use third-party communication service providers to send institution-related communications.

These communications may include:

* Attendance messages
* Fee reminders
* Examination information
* Notices
* Announcements
* Other institution-related messages

The information shared with these providers will be limited to what is reasonably necessary to deliver the communication.

## 12. Third-Party Service Providers

BGI may use third-party service providers to operate and maintain certain application features.

These may include providers for:

* Cloud hosting
* Push notifications
* SMS services
* WhatsApp messaging
* Email delivery
* Payment processing
* Security
* Crash reporting
* Technical infrastructure

Third-party providers may process information only as necessary to provide their services and may be subject to their own privacy policies.

## 13. Data Sharing

Buddha Group of Institutions does not sell or rent personal information.

Information may be accessible to authorized parties when necessary for legitimate educational or administrative purposes, including:

* Authorized BGI administrators
* Authorized teachers and staff
* Students
* Parents/guardians
* Service providers
* Payment service providers
* Communication service providers
* Government authorities where legally required

Access to information is intended to be controlled according to user roles and permissions.

## 14. Children's Privacy

BGI may process information relating to students who are children or minors.

The application is intended for use in an educational environment by students, parents/guardians, teachers, staff, and authorized administrators.

Student information is collected and processed for legitimate educational, administrative, safety, attendance, communication, and institutional purposes.

BGI does not sell children's personal information and does not intend to use student information for targeted advertising.

Parents/guardians or authorized institutional representatives may contact BGI regarding questions or requests concerning a student's personal information.

## 15. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information from unauthorized:

* Access
* Disclosure
* Modification
* Loss
* Misuse
* Destruction

Security measures may include:

* Secure authentication
* Role-based access controls
* Password protection
* Encrypted network communication
* Secure server infrastructure
* Access restrictions
* Monitoring and security controls

However, no internet-based service or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 16. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary for:

* Providing educational services
* Maintaining student records
* Maintaining attendance records
* Maintaining academic records
* Maintaining fee and payment records
* Providing institutional services
* Meeting administrative requirements
* Resolving disputes
* Maintaining security
* Complying with applicable laws and regulations

Certain educational and administrative records may need to be retained for longer periods based on institutional or legal requirements.

## 17. Data Deletion

Users may request deletion or correction of personal information where applicable.

However, certain records, including academic, attendance, fee, examination, and administrative records, may need to be retained where required for legitimate institutional or legal purposes.

To request account or personal-data deletion, please contact Buddha Group of Institutions using the contact information provided below.

We may require verification of the identity and authorization of the requester before processing a deletion request.

## 18. User Rights

Subject to applicable laws and institutional requirements, users may have the right to:

* Request access to personal information
* Request correction of inaccurate information
* Request deletion where legally permitted
* Ask about how information is being used
* Raise privacy-related concerns

Requests may be submitted to our official contact address.

## 19. Advertising

**BGI does not currently use personal information for targeted advertising and does not sell student or parent information to advertisers.**

If advertising functionality is introduced in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated as appropriate.

## 20. External Websites and Services

The BGI application may contain links or integrations to external websites or third-party services.

Buddha Group of Institutions is not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party websites or services that are not operated by BGI.

Users are encouraged to review the privacy policies of those third-party services.

## 21. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time because of changes to:

* Application functionality
* Institutional services
* Data-processing practices
* Security practices
* Legal or regulatory requirements

Any updated Privacy Policy will be made available through the BGI application or the official BGI website.

The **Effective Date** at the top of this Privacy Policy will indicate when the policy was last updated.
